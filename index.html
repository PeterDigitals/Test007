Import many questions at once by pasting them in the format below, or upload a CSV file.

Format (one question per block, blank line between):
Q: What is the capital of Nigeria?
A: Abuja
B: Lagos
C: Kano
D: Ibadan
ANS: A

Q: Water boils at ___°C
A: 50
B: 80
C: 100
D: 120
ANS: C
CSV columns (with header row):
question, optionA, optionB, optionC, optionD, answer

The "answer" column should be A, B, C, or D.
